## Future Interns — Data Science & Analytics Internship
**Task 2: Customer Retention & Churn Analysis**

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer churn in a telecommunications company dataset. The goal is to understand customer retention patterns and identify customer groups with higher churn rates using data analysis and visualization.

## Objectives
- Analyze customer churn and retention rates.
- Understand customer tenure and monthly charges.
- Compare churn across different contract types.
- Analyze internet services and payment methods.
- Explore the relationship between technical support and customer churn.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention.

## Dataset
The project uses the Telco Customer Churn dataset, which contains customer information, subscribed services, account details, and churn status.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

## Project Workflow
1. Loaded and explored the dataset.
2. Checked missing values, duplicates, and data types.
3. Cleaned the data and handled missing values.
4. Calculated churn and retention rates.
5. Analyzed customer tenure and monthly charges.
6. Compared churn across contract types, internet services, payment methods, and customer characteristics.
7. Created graphs and visualizations to understand churn patterns.
8. Developed business recommendations based on the analysis.

## Key Analysis
- Overall customer churn and retention.
- Churn by contract type.
- Churn by internet service and payment method.
- Customer tenure analysis.
- Monthly charges comparison.
- Technical support and customer characteristics analysis.

## Business Recommendations
- Improve onboarding and support for new customers.
- Offer suitable long-term plans to customers who may benefit from them.
- Monitor customer feedback and service issues.
- Review pricing and payment experiences.
- Track churn rates regularly to identify opportunities to improve retention.

## Conclusion
This project uses exploratory data analysis and data visualization to understand customer churn and retention patterns. The findings can help businesses identify customer groups that may need additional support and develop strategies to improve customer satisfaction and retention.
